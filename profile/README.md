## How We Work

Boldare operates on a **holacratic structure** — distributed authority instead of a traditional management hierarchy, letting teams move faster and closer to the client's problem.

We've also invested in internal AI fluency: our engineers hold **AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Associate (SAA-C03)** and complete **10xDevs**, an internal program on using AI responsibly in production — architectural decision-making with AI, context engineering, and evaluating models against real project constraints (cost, performance, risk).
